Arena scenery, reviewed 2026-11-26 (docs/ASSET_LOG.md A-0019 and A-0023).

1. Seventeen original 3D models by vinish.dev, CC BY 4.0.
   Source and publisher license: https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/
   License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/   License text: vinish-CC-BY-4.0.txt
   Publisher allows personal/commercial use, modifications and sharing with attribution and change notices.
   Files are unchanged (embedded glTF attribution and animation clips retained). Changes: runtime placement,
   rotation, uniform scale and simple gameplay colliders.

2. Thirteen Meshy community models, CC0 1.0 (license text: CC0-1.0.txt), by the creators named in
   scenery-sources.json, plus one remesh of the vinish.dev "Shipping Container" (CC BY 4.0, credited above).
   Each was remeshed on the project's Meshy account (task IDs recorded); base colour resized to 1024 px JPEG
   (the fuel drum 512 px) and the other maps to 512 px; provenance is written into each file's asset.copyright and asset.extras.
   Geometry, materials and nodes of the remesh are unchanged. Originals stay in assets_src/approved-art/scenery/.

Per-file titles, creators, listing URLs, hashes and evidence are in scenery-sources.json.
The game's visible Credits screen includes these attributions, generated from ASSET_LOG.md.
