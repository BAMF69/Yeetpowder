# Handoff: Yeetpowder gameplay mockup clips

## Your role
You are an art and video agent. Make **mockup gameplay video clips** for an unreleased game called **Yeetpowder**. They're concept visuals that show what the game *would* look like in play, for a pitch and to set the art direction.

**Do not build the game.** Don't write game code, set up an engine project, or make anything playable. Use whatever rendering, animation, 3D or video-generation tools you have to make clips that *look like* real gameplay footage.

## The game in one paragraph
Yeetpowder is a silly first-person party shooter that runs in a web browser. Every gun's recoil throws the shooter across the map. Shotguns blast you backward into walls, sniper rifles spin you like a top, and shooting at the floor launches you into the air. Up to 8 players brawl in short 5-minute matches. Think late-90s split-screen spy-shooter energy, but slapstick physics comedy.

**Lore:** yeetpowder is a cheap new propellant that burns hot pink and packs far more kick than guns were built for. Standard guns are ordinary guns overloaded with it: scuffed, taped-up and held together with zip ties. Gunblades are heavy, custom-forged, engraved weapons with recoil vents, built to channel the extra energy. Background details can include pink scorch marks and warning posters like "BRACE BEFORE YOU BLAST".

## Visual style
- **Look:** "N64 spy movie meets Saturday-morning cartoon." Chunky low-poly shapes, bright flat colors, simple textures, bold silhouettes.
- **Characters:** stubby, big-headed figures in color-coded jumpsuits (red, blue, yellow, green, purple, orange) with goggles and helmets. Their bodies flail and tumble when launched.
- **Effects:** oversized **hot-pink** muzzle flashes and pink smoke puffs (yeetpowder burns pink; never orange-yellow), comic-book text pops ("BLAM!", "YEET!", "BONK!"), speed lines on launches, cartoon stars on wall impacts, confetti-like hit sparks. **No blood or gore.**
- **HUD (mock, first-person clips):** chunky rounded UI. Bottom-left health pill, bottom-right ammo count, top-right kill feed with verbs like "YEETED", "BONKED", "REAPED", and a center crosshair. For mobile-format clips, show translucent virtual buttons: a left movement stick, plus right-side Fire, Jump and Slash buttons.
- **Palette:** saturated primaries on warm, sunny environments. Team colors must be colorblind-safe.

## Clip specs
- Length: 6–12 seconds each, looping cleanly where possible.
- Formats: 16:9 at 1920×1080 for every clip. Also 9:16 at 1080×1920 for clips 1, 4 and 6 (social and phone versions).
- 30 or 60 fps, MP4 (H.264). Sound effects and music are optional. If included: punchy cartoon gun sounds, slide-whistle launches, a crowd "ooooh", surf-rock or spy-jazz music.
- Camera: mostly first-person gameplay view. Clips marked *third-person* may use a spectator or replay camera.

## Shot list
1. **Shotgun Yeet (first-person):** a player in a retro spy-bunker hallway fires a shotgun at an opponent. The recoil blasts them backward down the corridor into a wall, with a "BONK!" pop and cartoon stars. The camera shakes and briefly goes crooked.
2. **Spin Doctor (first-person to third-person):** on a sunset rooftop, a player fires a sniper rifle across a gap and lands the hit ("YEETED" in the kill feed). The recoil sends the shooter spinning like a top. Cut to a third-person replay of the spin.
3. **Floor Launch (first-person):** a player aims straight down and fires a shotgun, rockets up to a high crate stack, and lands awkwardly.
4. **Gatling Copter killstreak (third-person, then first-person):** a big "GATLING COPTER!" banner appears. The player fires a gatling gun downward, lifts off and wobbles over a factory map like an out-of-control helicopter, raining bullets while everyone below shoots up. It ends with an overheat, a spinning crash landing and a cartoon dust cloud.
5. **Reaper's Kick gunblade (third-person replay):** a player with a weathered farm-scythe that has a sniper barrel built into the shaft fires downward, backflips high, and sweeps the scythe through an opponent on the way down ("REAPED"). The style is rustic and grim-reaper-ish: wood, rust, bone charms. **It must not resemble *RWBY*'s Crescent Rose:** no red-and-black, no sleek mechanical anime styling, no rose motifs.
6. **Six-Shot Sakura gunblade (first-person):** a player with a katana that has a revolver built into the handle does a fast 6-hit combo. Each revolver shot lunges them forward into the next slash, with muzzle flashes between cuts. It ends with a flashy spin-reload.
7. **Party chaos montage (third-person, wide):** 8 color-coded players on one map, all launching, tumbling and bonking at once, ending on a "YEET OF THE MATCH" replay title card.
8. **Phone vs. laptop (split screen):** the same match shown side by side as a phone (9:16 with touch buttons) and a laptop (16:9 with a keyboard HUD). The message is "same match, any device."

## Rules
- Original designs only. No existing game characters, logos, maps, weapons or UI (including GoldenEye, Perfect Dark, Halo, Fortnite, Team Fortress 2, *RWBY* or Final Fantasy gunblades). The inspiration is the *feel*, not the assets.
- No real brands, real people or real-world weapon logos.
- Cartoon violence only: no blood, gore or realistic injury.
- Put the "YEETPOWDER" logotype only on title cards: chunky, playful lettering with a small powder-keg or muzzle-flash motif.

## Process
1. First, send 3–4 still concept frames (one each for the shotgun yeet, Gatling Copter, Reaper's Kick and the character lineup) for approval before rendering motion.
2. After approval, render clips 1–8.
3. Deliver: one file per clip named `yeetpowder_01_shotgun-yeet_16x9.mp4` (and so on), a contact sheet image with one frame from each clip, and a short note per clip on the tools used and anything you changed from this brief.

If a requirement conflicts with what your tools can do, pick the closest workable option and note it. Don't skip the clip.
