Yeetpowder HUD concepts

The look comes from the title art and the menus: worn stencil paint on dark metal, diamond-plate tread on the plates, and hot pink only when something needs you right now. The HUD sits only at the corners, so the view stays open.

What's decided here

Type

Saira Stencil One for words (the same as the menus). Teko for every number, with tabular figures so ammo doesn't jitter. Black Ops One only for stamps. All three are already in the repo (OFL).

Material

Diamond-plate tread only on the two main plates, toned down to gunmetal. Small chips are plain metal so the texture never sits behind small text. Cut corners and rivets, no rounded boxes, no glow.

Colour

Cream does the informing. Pink is reserved for danger: low health, empty ammo, the hit marker, the damage wedge, the current slot underline. Green and yellow are gone.

Pops split three ways

Stamps (YEET, BONK, KABOOM): small, tilted, worn ink. They cascade out left and right and fade, three at most. Knockouts and pickups go to the feed. Notices such as "Launch camera on" become a small line under the crosshair.

Weapons

Desktop: an icon row of owned guns only, with key numbers. Phone: one chip with the current gun (tap it to open the picker) and a swipeable strip of owned guns at the bottom right, one tap to switch. The full names go away.

Phone footprint

Two 34 px chips at the top corners. That's about 6% of the screen, against roughly 35% today. Text scales with the ui_scale setting. The layout respects safe-area insets.
