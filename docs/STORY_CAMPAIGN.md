# Story & Tutorial Campaign: "Brace Before You Blast"

> Design draft, revised 2026-11-28 (twice) after Elaina's notes: each mini boss wields and drops its chapter's gunblade, the chapter boss needs it; purpose-built linear scenario maps (platformer
> style, skill jumps, gated by mastery), not the arena maps; regular mini bosses that show off the
> gunblades and combine the chapter's techniques; 4 chapters. Not yet scheduled (tier: Vertical slice):
> it sits in the ROADMAP Parking lot. Story revised 2026-11-28: set years after the invention; Smiths are
> the heroes, the Stuffers a lawless gang stealing their blades. Lore names are *proposals* for Elaina to keep, change or cut.

## The story (simple enough for a 12-year-old)
Story rule: short sentences, everyday words, one clear goal per chapter. Every line of story fits on a
poster or a two-line caption.

**The world.** Yeetpowder was invented years ago. It's cheap, pink and kicks so hard that any gun
throws you across the room. People have learned to live with it, and to fly with it.

**The good guys: the Smiths.** A guild of makers who spent years learning to tame yeetpowder. They
forge **gunblades**: blades with guns built in, made to turn the kick into flips, dashes and jumps.
Their three master blades are the **Sakura**, the **Reaper** and the **Kaboomerax**.

**The bad guys: the Stuffers.** A lawless gang who cram yeetpowder into old, taped-up guns. They're
loud, messy and they don't make anything. They want the Smiths' blades so they can copy them and rule
the island.

**What happens.** The Stuffers raid the Smiths' forge and steal the three master blades. You're a
new Smith apprentice. Your teacher, **Forgemaster Dot**, trains you, then sends you to get the blades
back, one per chapter, before the Stuffers learn to copy them.

| Chapter | Goal in one sentence |
|---|---|
| 1. The Forge Academy | Learn to shoot and fly, and win back the **Sakura** from the raiders. |
| 2. The Chop Shop | Chase the gang to their junkyard and win back the **Reaper**. |
| 3. The Copy Factory | Stop the Stuffers copying blades and win back the **Kaboomerax**. |
| 4. Fort Ducttape | Learn armour, use every blade, and beat the gang's boss. |

**The ending.** The Stuffer boss is yeeted off his own fort. The blades go back on the forge wall,
with your name added under them. "Now go yeet your friends!"

No twists, no long cutscenes: Dot says one or two short lines over the radio at the start of a room,
and posters and notes you find tell the rest. Funny, not scary: the Stuffers are clumsy, not cruel;
no blood, and every fail is a big rubber "FAILED" stamp and an instant retry.

## Map design rules (all chapters)
- **Purpose-built, linear, readable.** Short platformer rooms strung in a line (corridor → skill room
  → checkpoint), gray-box blocks with pink hazard stripes on anything you must recoil across. Not the
  arena maps; they share only the art kit.
- **One verb per room, then combine.** Room A teaches it safely (foam pit below), room B needs it
  with a little risk, room C mixes it with an earlier verb.
- **Gates by mastery, not text.** A gap only a downward shot can cross, a ledge only a wall-bonk
  reaches, a door that opens when a target is hit mid-air. One prompt on screen at most, showing the
  player's own bound key or touch/gamepad button.
- **Generous checkpoints** every room; falling = instant respawn at the room start (under 1 s).
- **Two routes per skill room:** a safe slow route for flailers, a skill route (marked with a pink
  arrow decal) that is faster and hides a **stolen blueprint page** collectible (a funny note + medal).
- **Mini boss every 2–3 rooms**, a full boss at chapter end. Bosses use only the moves taught so
  far, so a boss is a test, not a wall. Every boss has readable tells (a pink glow before an attack)
  and a 3-hit-phase health bar.
- **Length:** chapter about 10–15 minutes for a first-timer, 3–4 minutes for a speedrun (medal times).
- Campaign is optional: Quick Play is never locked. Chapter 1's first three rooms double as a
  90-second "Apprentice Basics" offered once from the menu.

## The gunblade spine (one per chapter)
Each chapter's **mini boss wields that chapter's gunblade**. Beat it and you **earn the blade**; the
rooms after the mini boss teach its moves, and **the chapter boss cannot be beaten without them**.
The guns from earlier rooms still matter, but the blade is the key.

| Ch. | Blade (earned from) | Moves you master | Why the chapter boss needs it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sakura** (Raider Rin) | Slash-fire combos, dash slash | Combos interrupt the boss's slow wind-ups; dash slash is the only way past his hazard sweeps |
| 2 | **Reaper** (Hookjaw) | Flips, hook and drag | Flips clear his floor hazards; hook-and-drag pulls his core out of armour to hit it |
| 3 | **Kaboomerax** (Big Bertha Boom) | Rocket jumps, grenade slashes | Rocket jumps dodge his attacks and climb the arena; grenade slashes surge you forward and break pursuing drones |
| 4 | *all three* + **armour** | Armour types and matchups | Boss swaps armour each phase; pick the right blade or gun for it |

## Chapter 1: The Forge Academy → win back the Sakura
*Theme: Smith training yard, wooden dummies, foam pits, warm forge light. Story: the raid starts mid-lesson; Dot keeps teaching while the Stuffers smash the place.*

| Room | Skill | Gate / challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 1-1 First Day | Walk, jump, look, pick up | Step-up blocks; pick up the Pea Pistol |
| 1-2 Little Nudges | Hip fire pushes you opposite your aim | Shoot backward to slide across ice to a door |
| 1-3 Hop Scotch | Shoot down to hop; double hop | Floating platforms over foam |
| 1-4 Brace Yourself | Aimed vs hip fire; reload timing | Small targets from a crumbling ledge; reload mid hop chain |
| **Mini boss: Raider Rin** (stolen Sakura) | Uses hop + braced fire | She can barely control the blade: she dashes and slash-combos; you hop over the dash and shoot her back. **Drops the Sakura.** |
| 1-5 Combo Drills | **Sakura slash-fire combos** | Training dummies wind up slow swings (pink glow); a combo staggers them before the swing lands |
| 1-6 Hazard Run | **Dash slash** | Sweeping laser bars and closing shutters, too fast to jump; dash slash carries you through the gap |
| **Chapter boss: Wrecking Rex** | Combos + dash slash | Stuffer brute in a taped-together crane suit with slow, telegraphed hammer swings: combo him mid-swing to stagger and open his core. Phase 2: he floods the floor with sweeping hazards; dash slash through them to reach the core. Reward: a map to the gang's junkyard. |

## Chapter 2: The Chop Shop (Stuffer junkyard) → win back the Reaper
*Theme: scrap piles, car crushers, conveyors, duct tape everywhere. Story: the gang is taking stolen Smith gear apart to see how it works.*

| Room | Skill | Gate / challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 2-1 Boomstick 101 | Shotgun self-launch | Blast the floor to climb a shaft |
| 2-2 Pinball Wizard | Wall bounces, two blasts in mid-air | Zig-zag chimney; long gap over crushers |
| 2-3 Spin Cycle | Spin Doctor twist, scope, zoom knock-out | Snipe switches on a turntable; snipe a guard off a far ledge |
| **Mini boss: Hookjaw** (stolen Reaper) | Uses launch + sniping, Sakura still useful | Hooks you toward a press: blast away from the hook, snipe him from a bounce perch, combo him when stunned. **Drops the Reaper.** |
| 2-4 Flip Course | **Reaper flips** | Floor of grinders, steam jets and electrified belts; flips clear them where a jump can't |
| 2-5 Hook Gym | **Hook and drag** | Hook crates onto pressure plates, drag guards off ledges into foam, hook-swing across gaps |
| **Chapter boss: The Crusher** | Flips + hook and drag | Stuffer junk-mech built around a car crusher. Its floor fills with hazard waves: flip over them. Its core sits behind armour shutters: **hook the core and drag it out**, then hit it. Phase 2: drag its feeder bots into its own press. Reward: you find out where the gang copies the blades. |

## Chapter 3: The Copy Factory → win back the Kaboomerax
*Theme: a noisy factory of bad blade copies that fall apart, sparks, scrap towers. Story: the Stuffers are making cheap fake gunblades; smash the line before the copies work.*

**Kaboomerax (already in the game, renamed from Rocket Axe 2026-11-28):** fires rockets *and*
grenades. Rocket at your feet = rocket jump; F + Fire = grenade slash (leap and forward surge that
blasts what it hits).

| Room | Skill | Gate / challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 3-1 Grenade Arc | Thrown grenades, aimed vs hip, trajectory guide | Lob grenades over walls onto switches |
| 3-2 Hold the Line | Yeet Shield, rear weakness; healing | Turret hallway; heal mid-gauntlet |
| **Mini boss: Big Bertha Boom** (stolen Kaboomerax) | Uses shield, grenades, Sakura, Reaper | She rocket jumps away and grenade-slashes at you: shield the slash, hook her down out of the air. **Drops the Kaboomerax.** |
| 3-3 Scrap Tower | **Rocket jumps** (climb, dodge) | Vertical tower; falling scrap must be dodged mid-climb |
| 3-4 Chase Yard | **Grenade slashes** (forward surge, destroy pursuers) | Swarms of fake-blade drones chase you down a long corridor; surge forward and smash them |
| **Chapter boss: The Copycat** (the gang's inventor) | Rocket jumps + grenade slashes | Collapsing factory tower. His ground slams and molten sweeps are dodged only by rocket jumping, and climbing to the next tier needs rocket jumps too. Phase 2: he sends copy-drones after you; grenade-slash through them to reach him. Reward: all three blades are back; time to end the gang. |

## Chapter 4: Fort Ducttape → armour and everything together
*Theme: a fort of shipping containers and tape on an island, shark moat, rising pink powder spill. Story: the gang wore stolen Smith armour; beat their boss and take it all back.*

**Armour (from the design doc's Future systems):** damage types Bullet / Slash / Blast; armour
None, Modern vest, Medium, Chainmail, Plate. Vests shrug off bullets, plate shrugs off slashes, heavy
armour can't escape by recoil, so blasts, ledges and ring outs beat it. Visible at a glance: clanks
and sparks on deflected slashes, a dull thud on bullets into vests.

| Room | Skill | Gate / challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 4-1 Read the Armour | Damage types | Stuffer guards in vests, chainmail and plate; one weapon fails on each, a tooltip shows why once |
| 4-2 Heavy Problem | Heavy armour weakness: knockback, ledges, sharks | Plate-wearing guards can't be cut: hook them into the shark pond or blast them off ledges |
| 4-3 Suit Up | Wearing armour yourself and the recoil trade-off | Plate makes a crossing safe but stops your launch; swap at stations to clear each part |
| 4-4 Copter Up | Gatling Copter: hover, overheat | Shaft climb with overheat vents |
| **Mini boss: The Tape Twins** | All three blades vs two armours | One in plate with a Reaper, one in a vest with a Sakura: shoot the plate one, slash the vest one, or hook one into the other's blast |
| 4-5 Rising Tide | Everything, timed | Climb ahead of the rising pink flood; skill routes use every blade |
| **Final boss: King Kludge, the Stuffer boss** | All moves + armour matchups | Wears every piece of stolen armour in a recoil-powered suit that swaps armour each phase. P1 vest: Sakura combos and dash slash past sweeps. P2 chainmail: Reaper flips over hazards, hook-drag his core. P3 plate: rocket jumps to dodge, grenade slashes and blasts (blast beats plate). P4 armour shattered, he flees on his fort's giant cannon: Gatling Copter chase, finish with a Yeetreap off the cannon. |

**Epilogue (15 s):** the three blades go back on the forge wall with your name under them. "Now go yeet your friends!"

## Boss design rules
- Mini boss = the blade's showcase. The player sees every move it will teach before owning it.
- Chapter boss = the blade's exam. It must be **unbeatable without the chapter's blade moves**, but
  forgiving with them; guns from that chapter help, never replace.
- Readable tells: pink glow 0.6 s before an attack, a sound cue, a caption.
- Funny failures: hooked into a press, launched by your own grenade. Reuse `HighlightRecorder` for "Yeet of the Fail".
- Three medals per boss (time / no heals / no hits).

## Build cost and reuse
New system this needs: **armour** with damage types (also affects Party mode: decide with an ADR
whether it ships there). The Kaboomerax already exists.
Reuses: weapons, gunblades, bots' brain (as boss pattern controllers), dummies, sharks, HighlightRecorder,
captions, tuning in `src/core/yeet/tuning.ts`. New: a linear level format (rooms, checkpoints, gates,
triggers) in data files, a boss pattern script runner, ~30 radio lines for Dot, poster decals.
**Prototype first:** Chapter 1 rooms 1-3, Raider Rin, room 1-6 and Wrecking Rex (one full blade arc), in gray box, then playtest
before building the rest.
